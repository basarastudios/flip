About the Game

Flip! is a fast, one-tap casual game where timing is everything. Charge your jump, perform smooth flips, and land perfectly to build combos. Easy to play, hard to master.

Features

  • Simple one-tap controls
  • Satisfying flip physics
  • Perfect landing combos
  • Quick, addictive sessions
  • Minimal, colorful design
